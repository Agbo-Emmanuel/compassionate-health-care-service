Live-In-Care

Our Live-In-Care support is a covenient alternative to a senior living community and independent living or assisted living facility. it provides many of the same benefits while allowing your loved one to enjoy life from home. Designed to care for those who need personal assistance during the day but typically sleep through the nights. This service option is a great solution for seniors who live alone and will benefit from companionship and home maker services, as well as personal care

Our live-in caregivers are capable of the following:

  • Providing Companionship and engagement
  • Light housekeeping
  • Fall prevention
  • Errand assistance
  • Medication reminder
  • Morning and bedtime assistance
  • Personal care and hygiene
  • And more depending on the level of care needed
